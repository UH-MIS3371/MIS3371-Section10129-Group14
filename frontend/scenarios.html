    Your goal

    You start with $100,000. Make a profit for one star, beat the bot as well for two, and beat the market too for three.

    1. Pick a stock in the watchlist to see its chart.
    2. Buy if you think it will rise, Short if you think it will fall. Close a position from the Positions tab.
    3. Watch the headlines. The game pauses when big news breaks so you can react.

    How the bot works

    The bot is a trend follower. It never reads the headlines; it only looks at prices. It starts with the same $100,000 as you, trades at the same real prices and only buys whole shares.

    1. Every day at the close it works out each stock's 20-day average: the average of its last 20 closing prices.
    2. Sell. Any stock it owns that closed below its average is sold.
    3. Buy. Stocks that closed above their average, and that it does not own yet, are bought. Each stock gets at most an equal share of its money (), so when few stocks are rising, most of its money stays in cash.

    How to beat it

    A trend follower is always a step late: it buys after a rise has started and sells after a fall has begun. Sudden turns, like a crash followed by a fast rebound, catch it out. Reading the headlines and acting before the trend shows up is your edge. You can also short a stock to profit from a fall; the bot never does.
