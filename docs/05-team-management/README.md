# Team participation tracker

[Open the tracker](team-tracker.xlsx).

The workbook has three sheets:

- **Participation**: team members down the left, one column per class activity, group exercise or milestone, in date order from September 3 to November 30. Scores run from 0 to 100. Click a score to change it.
- **Evidence**: each member's commits on `main` per week, taken from the repository history.
- **Date key**: the date of every column and where that date comes from (the Canvas due dates and the instructor's posts).

Milestone 3 is the November 1 column, Milestone 4 is the November 22 column, and the Final Team Presentation is the November 30 column. Its scores stay blank until the presentation is done.

Names and headers stay visible when you scroll right. To add a future activity, use the next column to the right and add its date to the Date key sheet.
